ABOUT KONIFA

柯尼发（上海）仓储设备有限公司，成立于2023年，位于上海市，是一家专注于仓储设备与物流产品的工程企业。Founded in 2023 in Shanghai, Konifa (Shanghai) Storage Equipment Co., Ltd. is an engineering company focused on storage equipment and logistics products.

公司主要生产销售各种钢货架、钢结构平台等仓储设备和仓储物流产品，服务制造业、冷链冷库、汽配、电商、粮油、建材与第三方物流等行业。We manufacture and supply steel racking, steel platforms and other storage and logistics products for manufacturing, cold chain, automotive, e-commerce, grain and oil, building materials and third-party logistics.

从方案布局、荷载验算、图纸设计，到生产、安装和终身维保，项目团队按安全优先的原则逐环节把关，为客户提供完整的仓储设备工程服务。From layout, structural calculation and drawings to production, installation and long-term maintenance, our project team checks every stage with safety first to deliver complete storage equipment engineering.
